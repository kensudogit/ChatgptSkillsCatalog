# PCB Design Checklist

This skill guides PCB design review checkpoints.

## Checklist

1. Land / clearance matches internal design rules
2. Decoupling capacitors are near power pins
3. High-speed impedance control and length matching
4. Thermal vias / copper area meet thermal requirements
5. Silk / polarity markings are readable

## How to use

Provide the design under review and list findings against the checklist above.
